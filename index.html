Taste the Flavors of Nepal.

Enjoy authentic Nepali dishes prepared with fresh, local ingredients. From traditional Dal Bhat to steaming Momo — taste the warmth of home-cooked Nepali food.

Dal Bhat Tarkari

The heart of Nepali cuisine — rice, lentil soup, vegetable curry, and pickles. A complete, satisfying meal.

Momo

Freshly steamed dumplings served with spicy tomato achar. A beloved Nepali favorite.

Nepali Chowmein

Stir-fried noodles cooked with fresh local vegetables, fragrant mountain spices, and mild chili paste.

Himalayan Thukpa

A soothing, warming noodle soup simmered with garden vegetables, ginger, and aromatic Himalayan herbs.

Nepali Spiced Chiya & Coffee

Freshly brewed milk tea infused with cardamom, clove, and cinnamon, alongside fresh morning coffee.

Seasonal Local Snacks

Freshly fried Palpali batuk, chukauni, local potato snacks, and seasonal garden greens.

Discover Palpa.

Palpa is one of the most beautiful districts in western Nepal, known for its rolling green hills, traditional Newari architecture, and rich cultural heritage. The Ramdi area offers a peaceful escape into nature with stunning views and a slower pace of life.

From the guest house, you can explore the surrounding countryside, visit local temples, and experience the authentic daily life of the Nepali hills.

Ramdi Bridge & River

A scenic spot near the guest house — enjoy the views of the river and surrounding hills.

Tansen & Palpa Bazaar

Explore the historic town of Tansen, known for its traditional architecture and local markets.

Local Temples & Culture

Visit nearby temples and experience the traditions and festivals of the Palpa region.
